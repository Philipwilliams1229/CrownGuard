## Questions

1. **Mood.** Iron today is cold turf, paved road, crags, war camps. Should the overhaul push it toward
   (a) a hard military march: quarried stone, drilled camps, banners, little wild growth; (b) high
   moorland: heather, gorse, wind-bent trees, the army as an intruder; (c) a mix by level (camps near the
   Citadel, moor on the early levels)?
2. **Trees.** Fewer and darker than the Greenwood (Scots pine, spruce, wind-bent thorn)? Should Iron
   have a season of its own (the owner loved autumn in the concepts): late autumn, bare thorn and gold
   larch, against the Greenwood's summer?
3. **Water.** Iron's rivers run under stone: keep the Greenwood's four edge kinds, or give Iron its own
   (walled stone embankments and quays near camps, rocky gorge banks on the moor)? Rivers may be wider
   and repathed (the owner allowed it): is Iron the chapter to try a truly wide river?
4. **Bridges.** The humpback is picked. Variety inside it: a plain packhorse bridge, a fortified bridge
   with a gate tower, a ruined span patched with timber? The up-the-screen rule applies as it stands.
5. **Height.** Crags and cliff edges were liked. Iron is where terraces / scarps would look most at
   home ("A looks cool on rimewater"): try them here in the concepts, or keep them parked?
6. **Places.** What lives beside the road: the war camp (tents, pickets, forge, cook fires), a toll
   fort, a quarry, a beacon hill, a drover's inn? Which should come back on several levels as Iron's
   "farm"?
7. **Road.** Paving flags today. Worn cart ruts were rejected on the Greenwood road ("don't make the
   lanes in the road so obvious"): does that hold for a paved military road too?
8. **Light.** Iron levels already carry a planned sun each (afternoon, late, golden, sunrise...). Camp
   fires, forge glow and beacons as the area's lamps: lit at every hour, or only when the sun is low?
9. **Storms.** Iron's weather is the thunderstorm, with flooded road stretches and puddles. Should the
   new ground and water be drawn so the storm reads stronger (darker stone when wet, streams in spate)?
10. **Order of work.** Same as the Greenwood: one board first for the owner to play (suggested:
    Ironford, the river-and-bridge board), then the rest? And should the campaign map's Iron region wait
    until the boards are done?
