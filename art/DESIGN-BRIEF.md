# Crownguard — art brief for separate sprite PNGs

I'm attaching the game's current sprite sheet (crownguard-sprite-sheet.png).
It shows every creature, soldier, crew figure, tower and piece of scenery in
Crownguard, a Kingdom Rush / Bloons-style tower defense for iPad (landscape).
The current art is generated by code out of overlapping circles and blobs. I
want it redrawn as REAL pixel art, delivered as separate PNG files, one per
animation frame, so I can drop them straight into the game.

Keep every character and building recognisably the same thing as on the
sheet (same colours, same gear, same size), but make it proper pixel art:
clear silhouettes, real faces, real hands on real weapons, no overlapping
circles.

## The style (do not change)

- Pixel art, drawn at the exact pixel sizes listed below. Whole square pixels
  only: no smoothing, no anti-aliasing, no gradients, no blur, no upscaling.
- Cel shading: exactly three tones per surface (light, mid, dark). The light
  comes from the upper left. Highlights lean warm cream (#fff3d2), shadows
  lean cool plum (#2a1c2c).
- A 2-pixel dark outline (#241a26) around the whole silhouette. Inside it,
  1-pixel dark lines only where big parts meet (arm against torso, roof
  against wall). Small details are shown with colour, not lines.
- Warm, saturated palette. Reference colours: grass #82b256, castle stone
  #a19a8a, roofs #a8505c, gold trim #d8b34a, steel #c4c8d0, oak #7a5334.
- Proportions: slim, grounded figures about 4 heads tall. NOT chibi, NOT
  big-headed. Soldiers look like people, goblins like small mean people,
  monsters like monsters.
- Camera: three-quarter top-down. We see a little of the top of things and
  the front of them. Every figure faces RIGHT. The game mirrors them to face
  left, so nothing should be written or asymmetric in a way that would look
  wrong mirrored.
- Transparent background. A soft ground shadow is fine as part of the image
  (a flat dark ellipse at 30% under the feet).

## File rules

- One PNG per frame. Name: `<name>_<sheet>_<frame>.png`, e.g.
  `goblin_walk_0.png`, `goblin_fight_1.png`. Variants add a word:
  `goblin_revived_walk_0.png`.
- Every frame of one creature is the SAME canvas size (given below), and the
  figure's feet stand on the same "feet line" in every frame, centred left to
  right. That is how the game lines them up. Fliers hover: their feet line is
  where their shadow falls; the body floats above it.
- Walk cycle = 4 frames: 0 contact (front foot down, arms swung one way),
  1 pass (legs together, body a pixel higher), 2 contact (other foot), 3
  pass. Same figure in every frame, just the limbs moving. A little bob is
  good; a redraw of the character is not.
- Fight = 2 frames: 0 ready (weapon raised, braced), 1 strike (weapon swung
  through, a lean of 2-3 pixels forward). Ranged foes: 0 aiming, 1 loosing.
- Fliers (bat, wraith, gryphon, dragon, eagle): "walk" is a 4-frame flap
  cycle (up, mid, down, mid). Fight = 0 ready, 1 bite/claw/breath.
- Beasts (wolf, ghoul, boarrider, cavalier, wolfrider): a 4-frame lope
  (gather, stretch, gather, stretch). Riders stay seated.

## The towers

Each tower has 9 forms: level 1, level 2, level 3, branch a, final aa,
final ab, branch b, final ba, final bb (left to right on the sheet). For
each form I want TWO things, so the crew can keep animating:

1. The building alone, no people on it: `<kind>_<form>.png`, 216 x 252
   pixels, the footing on the ground at 182 px from the top, centred.
   Rule: the crew must never be roofed in. The place they stand is open.
   Upgrades read from what is BEHIND and AROUND that spot: battlements,
   banners, bigger engines, a glow. Kinds: archer, knight, wizard, support
   (Warden Mage), catapult, spiker (Bladewheel), goldworks, trapsmith,
   falconry, gunpowder, riverwatch, assassin, sunforge.
2. The crew who stand on it, as separate figures at 40 x 52 pixels, feet
   52, two frames: `<name>_ready.png` and `<name>_act.png` (loosing, casting,
   hammering, throwing). The crew figures are the "Crew figures" row of the
   sheet: archer (base, branch a, branch b, crossbow), crew (winch man),
   stander, mage (level 1 and level 3), priest, smith, hooded blade,
   falcon mistress, bomber, musketeer, wall bowman, halberdier, mason.

## Scenery (optional, after the above)

Transparent PNGs, feet line at the bottom edge: tree (4 different trees,
120 x 130), pine (4, 90 x 150), rock (4, 100 x 70), deadtree, willow,
gravestone, cairn, boneheap, tent, mushroom, crystal. Crooked and natural,
never perfectly round. Trees: don't outline each leaf lobe fully, only
underline the bottom of each lobe.

## Where to start

In this order, because it's what a player sees in the first ten minutes:
goblin, wolf, orc, knight, heroKnight, heroHunter, farmer, then the Archer
Tower, Knight Garrison, Wizard Spire and Warden Mage with their crews, then
the trees. Send those first and I'll check they fit before you do the rest.
