Front and Back Views

Connecting the notes…

Every character that walks the road, as the game shows it: side (walking across), front (walking down the screen, toward you) and back (walking up it). They switch views mid-stride, and turn side-on whenever they fight. Open Frames under a figure to see each step. Leave a note under any figure (pick what it's about, write, Send): Claude reads them and answers right on the note.
