# Iron overhaul concepts, round 1, panel 4: CRAGS (2026-11-28)
Painter `concept/ovi1-crags.js`, lab `ovi1-crags.html` (`?board=greyhelm|blackcliff&opt=today|A|B|C&save=1`, `&sheet=1`, `&loop=heath|road&box=x,y,w,h`).
Real engine + draw() on Greyhelm (afternoon) and Blackcliff (golden). The board's own crags/heather/walls are lifted; ground work, rocks and road dressing are painted on a bare-board draw and the halls and foes laid back over them. Rocks follow THE ROCK RULE (stacked loaves, lit top, south face, dithered east shade, no peaks). Flat ground throughout: no terraces.
- A THE TORS (leans into the owner's reference photo): pale grey granite woolpack ridges, crimson/olive shrub carpet right up to the rocks, a dry stream run of tumbled boulders with gravel; road: old flags with a few paler new-flag patches, weeds, faint worn hollows.
- B THE CLIFF EDGE: a long gritstone escarpment (two staggered courses, a gully, scree, rust streaks, heather lip) plus bedded outcrops; road kept, patched, kerbstones on the verge, two puddles.
- C THE QUARRIED CRAG: dark slate, natural west loaf and a square-cut east half (courses, drill pits), dressed blocks, spoil; road RE-LAID in unequal slate flags with a dressed kerb (Citadel-end look). Replaces the road's chevrons.
Stills: `.shots/ovi1_crags_<greyhelm|blackcliff>_<today|A|B|C>.png` (today for blackcliff and greyhelm), rock ladders `ovi1_crags_sheet_<A|B|C>.png` (A also a 124-px tor, B the wall, C the quarry crag and dressed blocks).
Loops: `ovi1_anim_crags_heath_c6r4_fps8.png` (+`_still`; A at golden hour, heather/bracken/straw tufts stirring; step 242 px, seam 257 px, shimmer 16) and `ovi1_anim_crags_road_c6r4_fps8.png` (B, wind on tufts + puddle ripple/glint; stats not read).
Cost: rocks bake 2-10 ms each once, cached per size/seed/sun; ~25 sprites a board; live tufts are fillRect per px (~150 tufts, <0.2 ms); road mask is 470k nearestOnPath calls in the lab, in the game it would be baked into the ground layer once. Engine needs: rock `footprints` (ellipses) for `buildableAt`, per-board crag layouts, road dressing in the road bake.
Weak spots: tors read a little bun/beehive; A's greyhelm stream run barely shows; B's wall reads as shelves rather than a cliff (no raised ground by design); C's crag looks like masonry, dark road is heavy and drops the chevrons; loop frames show the halls' range rings (lab artefact) and tuft motion is small at 1x; puddles read as orange blobs at golden hour; NOT CHECKED: Crowstair, other suns, board-size (840) view of every still beyond the montage, shimmer count in the road loop.
My pick: A (the owner's photo; pale rock lifts off the dark wood) with B's long edge on the Pass boards and C's quarry only near the Citadel.
