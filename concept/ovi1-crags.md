Real engine + draw() on Greyhelm (afternoon) and Blackcliff (golden). The board's own crags/heather/walls are lifted; ground work, rocks and road dressing are painted on a bare-board draw and the halls and foes laid back over them. Rocks follow THE ROCK RULE (stacked loaves, lit top, south face, dithered east shade, no peaks). Flat ground throughout: no terraces.
- A THE TORS (the direction; the owner's reference photo): grey granite woolpack ridges, pale only on top, flat tops, one vertical joint and one bedding crack a loaf, uneven heights; solid low crimson/olive shrub clumps with a dark base in three drifts; a 26 px gravel stream bed with a few big boulders.
- B THE ERRATICS: no stacked tors; single boulders of every size strewn through the shrub carpet, loose lines, shrub drifts between.
- C THE CRAG LINE: a broken line of tors along the wood edge as backdrop, fallen boulders and shrub at its foot (on flat ground, no shelf).
- Road in every option: today's flags worn and patched, weeds, kerbstones and loose stones at the verge, two cool irregular puddles. (Pass 1's cliff wall, quarry crag and slate re-lay are cut; the rock-ladder sheets are not published.)
