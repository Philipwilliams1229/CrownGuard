What the light costs

Measured just now on the device you are holding, on a canvas the size of the game board.

…Measuring.

Nothing about the light is worked out while you play. Each drawing of a figure gets a second, see-through drawing made ahead of time that holds only the lit edges. Lighting a figure is stamping that second drawing on top, and the flicker is just how strongly it is stamped. So the cost is one extra stamp per lit figure, only while something is burning near it.

My recommendation: build it in, on by default, and still add a "Spell light" switch to the Display tab in Settings. The switch costs almost nothing to make and gives an old phone a way out.
