Crownguard · sprite concepts · round 1

Same size, better pixels

Six figures redrawn in a new way: sculpted pixel by pixel instead of drawn as shapes and hardened. Bigger, readable heads and faces, one-pixel outlines in each material's own dark, five-tone shading, twice the frames or more, and spells that light the caster.

What I need from you for round 2

  1. Proportions. These stand about 3.8 heads tall; today's figures are 4.5 and the style guide says "never big-headed". The bigger head is what makes the faces readable. Keep it, go further toward your reference (about 3.3), or pull back?
  2. Outline. A, B or C.
  3. Light. Keep one sun for both facings? Keep spell light, and should towers and torches cast it too?
  4. Which of the six is closest to what you want, and which is furthest? That tells me where to push.
