Crownguard — Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Crownguard is a single-player tower defense game. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information about you.

What the game does not do

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No contact with any server while you play. The app works fully offline.

Your saved game

Your progress and settings are saved only on your own device. On iPhone and iPad they are part of your device's normal backup, which is controlled by you and Apple, not by us. Deleting the app deletes the save.

The game can show you a "save code" (Settings → Progress) so you can move your progress to another device. That code is only ever copied or shared when you choose to do it.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the change reaches the game, and the date above will change.
